HOW PREMIUM MATCHING WORKS

Buy access, not individual calls.

Coins unlock time-based matching passes. Your conversations remain free once a real person connects.

0112-hour preference passGender, country, level or priority stays active for 12 hours and can be used across multiple real matches.
0224-hour Smart MatchCombine gender + country + speaking level in one stronger pass for 24 hours.
03No match, no chargeIf nobody suitable is online, your coins stay safe. The pass starts only after a successful human match.
04No call limitThere is no 5-minute or one-call coin limit. Match as many real people as the active pass allows during its validity.
💡Example: 4 coins can unlock your preferred gender for 12 hours — not just one conversation. 8 coins can unlock Smart Match for 24 hours.
