# Open Talk coin economy (v1)

Anchor: 100 coins = $0.99. 1 coin = 3 minutes of gender or country choice.

Earn: daily login 1 (+4 on every 7th day), rewarded video 1 (max 10 a day), achievements (one-time),
welcome gift 10. Spend: 30 min = 10 coins, 2 h = 35, 5 h = 80 (gender or country).
Minutes are used only while talking with the choice on; calls under 30 s are free.
A conversation counts toward achievements only if it lasts 3+ minutes.
Rule of thumb: 10 videos = 30 min, 20 videos (2 days) = 1 hour.
Coin packs (Play Billing, later): 100 / 550 / 1200 coins for $0.99 / $4.99 / $9.99.
