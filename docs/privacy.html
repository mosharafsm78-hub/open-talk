Open Talk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Open Talk helps people practice English by talking live with other users. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Profile details: the name, country and English level you choose.
  • Usage details: when calls start and end, call length, ratings, blocks and reports.

Microphone and voice calls

The app uses your microphone only during a call so your partner can hear you. Calls are delivered in real time through Agora. We do not record or store call audio.

How we use information

To create your account, match you with partners, show your daily speaking time, keep the community safe (reports and blocks), and improve the app.

Sharing

Your partner sees your name, country and English level during a call. We do not sell your data. Service providers (Supabase for accounts and data, Agora for real-time audio) process data on our behalf.

Safety

Open Talk is for users aged 18 and over. You can report or block any user after a call. Reports are reviewed and accounts that break the rules may be removed.

Your choices and account deletion

You can edit your profile and delete your account at any time in the app under Settings, then Delete account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and call history. You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.
