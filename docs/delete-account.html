Delete your Open Talk account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Open Talk and log in.
  2. Tap the settings icon on the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can no longer use the app, email mosharafsm78@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Open Talk account". We will delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile (name, country, gender, English level), coin balance, call history, ratings, blocks and reports you made are permanently removed. Call audio is never stored. Reports made about other users may be kept for safety reasons without your personal details.
