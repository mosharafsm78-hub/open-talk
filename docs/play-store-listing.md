# Open Talk - Google Play listing

**App name (max 30):** Open Talk: Speak English
**Short description (max 80):** Practice English by talking live with real people. Free to start.

**Full description:**
Open Talk helps you speak English with confidence by talking to real people, live.

Tap Start Talking and we connect you with someone who is online right now. No scripts, no pressure: just a friendly voice conversation, with a topic suggestion whenever you need one.

WHY OPEN TALK
- Real conversations: practice with real people from around the world, matched by availability.
- Made for learners: choose your level (beginner, intermediate or advanced) and get topics that fit it.
- Build a habit: a daily speaking goal, a streak and achievements keep you motivated.
- Choose who you talk to: with coins you can pick the gender or the country of your partner. Coins are free to earn: daily rewards, achievements and optional rewarded videos.
- Safe community: 18+ only, report and block anyone after a call, and calls are never recorded.
- Light and fast: a small app that works well on everyday phones and networks.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Join with your email and tell us your name, country and level.
2. Tap Start Talking.
3. Speak, laugh, learn. Rate the conversation when you finish.

Open Talk is for adults aged 18 and over. Contains optional rewarded video ads.
