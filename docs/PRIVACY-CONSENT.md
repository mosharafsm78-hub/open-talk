# Privacy and consent

Open Talk should use explicit consent before recording or transcribing a conversation. Voice data should be processed only for the stated purpose, retained for the minimum necessary period, and deletable by the user. Do not infer protected characteristics from voice or conversation. Provide clear controls to leave a conversation, block/report a partner, delete an account, and request deletion of eligible stored conversation data.

The AI level is a coaching estimate, not a medical, employment, education-admission, or other high-stakes decision.
