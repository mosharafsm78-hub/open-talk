Delete your account

Last updated: October 7, 2026

You can permanently delete your Open Talk account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Open Talk and sign in.
  2. Tap your account name at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email mosharafsm78@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile and sign-in details.
  • Your conversation history, practice progress, milestones, coins and passes.
  • Reports and blocks linked to your account.

What may remain

Encrypted database backups are kept for up to 14 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup for that period before the backup is removed. We do not record calls, so there is no call audio to delete.
