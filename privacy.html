Open Talk

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Open Talk is a spoken-English practice service for adults. It connects you by voice with other real people. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who can use Open Talk

Open Talk is only for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not allow anyone under 18 to create or keep an account. Open Talk is not directed at children.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider), and your name.
  • Age check: your date of birth and age, used to confirm you are 18 or over.
  • Profile and preferences: country, gender, English level, and who you prefer to be matched with.
  • Activity: when you search for a partner, who you were matched with, call start and end times and duration, coins, passes, milestones and practice progress.
  • Safety: reports you make or that are made about you, and the people you have blocked.
  • Technical data: information your device and network send automatically (such as IP address) when you connect to our services.

Voice calls

Calls use peer-to-peer WebRTC audio between you and your partner. We do not record your calls and we do not store your conversation audio. When a direct connection is not possible, your audio is passed through a relay server operated by a third party solely to carry it between you and your partner; it is not stored. Because we do not record calls, we rely on user reports and account activity to enforce our rules.

The app needs your microphone permission to make calls. You can turn it off in your device settings, but you will not be able to take part in calls.

How we use information

  • To run the service: sign you in, match you with partners, and keep your progress.
  • To keep people safe: confirm age, handle reports and blocks, and suspend accounts that break our rules.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — authentication, database and realtime messaging.
  • A TURN relay provider — carries call audio when a direct connection fails.
  • Website hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Netlify) and Google Fonts — deliver the web pages and fonts.

These providers process technical information under their own policies.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open your account (top of the screen), choose Delete my account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile and the data linked to your account, including conversation history, progress, coins, passes, reports and blocks. See how to delete your account.

Encrypted backups of our database are kept for up to 14 days, so deleted data can remain in backups for that period before they are removed.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your information, or for it to be corrected or deleted.

Security

Data is protected by access rules in our database so that you can only reach your own information, and by encrypted connections. No service is perfectly secure, so please do not share passwords, financial details or other sensitive information with a conversation partner.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app.
