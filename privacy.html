Open Talk

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Open Talk is a conversation-practice service designed to help people speak with real people and track practice progress.

Information we use

Depending on the features you use, Open Talk may process your profile name, age, country, gender, speaking level, matching preferences, account identifier, match/call information, and safety reports. Some practice statistics are stored locally on your device.

Voice conversations

Open Talk uses peer-to-peer WebRTC audio for live conversations. The current frontend does not record or upload your conversation audio. Do not share sensitive personal information during a conversation.

Matching and safety

Profile information is used to find compatible speaking partners. Safety reports may be stored so that abuse and policy issues can be reviewed.

Third-party services

Open Talk currently uses Supabase for authentication, database and realtime infrastructure, and Google Fonts/CDN resources for the web interface. These services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Data choices

You can request correction or deletion of account information by contacting the Open Talk team. We may retain safety records where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, fraud, or security incidents.

Children

Open Talk is not intended for children under 13. Users must provide truthful age information and should not use the service if they are below the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Open Talk team through the developer contact address associated with the app.
