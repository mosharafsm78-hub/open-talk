Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Open Talk has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page sets out our standards and how to report a concern.

Open Talk is for adults only

  • Open Talk is for people aged 18 and over. Children are not allowed to use it.
  • We ask for a date of birth at sign-up and refuse anyone under 18. This is checked in the app and enforced again on our server, so it cannot be bypassed by editing the app.
  • Existing accounts must confirm a date of birth before they can be matched.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove it.

What is not allowed

We prohibit any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits, grooms or endangers a child, including attempting to contact a minor, and sharing or requesting child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Anyone who does this is permanently banned.

How you can report

  • In the app: use Report during any call. You can also Block a partner so you are never matched again.
  • By email: mosharafsm78@gmail.com

What we do when we receive a report

  • We review reports and suspend accounts while we investigate.
  • We permanently remove accounts that break these standards.
  • Where we find or are made aware of child sexual abuse material or child exploitation, we report it to the appropriate authorities as the law requires, and we cooperate with law enforcement.

A limit you should know about

Calls are not recorded, so we cannot listen to what was said after the fact. We rely on reports from users and on account-level information. Please report anything you see or hear.

Child-safety contact

Our designated contact for child-safety matters: mosharafsm78@gmail.com
